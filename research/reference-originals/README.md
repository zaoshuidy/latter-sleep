## Lulu A5 template bundle

- Source: `https://assets.lulu.com/media/templates/book/lulu-book-template-all-a5.zip`
- Provider page: `https://www.lulu.com/publishing-toolkit`
- Local archive: `lulu-book-template-all-a5.zip`
- SHA-256: `B604553285B3C811350F34D499377D63E74B9ACFBBD7524FFA4D5871F304A243`
- Contents verified locally: A5 interior single-page and spread INDD/IDML templates, cover templates for multiple bindings, PDF export presets, and the provider's creation guide.

Extracted working copies are intentionally ignored by Git. The original ZIP remains the immutable evidence artifact.

These files are used to study production geometry, parent-page structure, bleed, document intent, and export settings. They are not a visual design license and must not be copied into final books without independent rights review.
